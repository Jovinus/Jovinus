## Hello, I'm Kyung Hyun 👋

I build clinical AI that can survive the journey from a research notebook to real-world care. My work spans **longitudinal health modeling**, **multicenter validation**, and **software as a medical device (SaMD)**.

I'm an AI Researcher at [AITRICS](https://www.aitrics.com), a Digital Health PhD student at [SAIHST, Sungkyunkwan University](https://www.saihst.kr/), and co-founder of [BreathYou](https://breathyou.care), where we turn respiratory AI research into clinician-facing products.
